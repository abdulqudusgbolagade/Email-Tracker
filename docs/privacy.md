# Privacy, retention and recipient requests

Grant Publishing Co. controls the outreach purpose, recipient selection and legal basis. Establish applicable consent/legitimate-interest requirements, identify the business accurately, keep outreach relevant and honor opt-outs. This software does not establish legal compliance by itself.

Tracking defaults to off. Enable only when justified for the recipients and jurisdictions involved. An image request may be generated by a privacy proxy, scanner, cache or automated client; a missing signal is equally inconclusive. The UI never describes a signal as a confirmed read or uses it alone to score a prospect.

## Data locations

- SQLite holds prospects, email bodies, immutable outbound MIME, history, reminders, encrypted mailbox credentials and audit entries on the owner's machine/server.
- The encryption key lives outside source control in local environment configuration. Credential encryption is not whole-database encryption; use filesystem controls and disk encryption for the complete CRM.
- D1 holds token hashes, event IDs, timestamps, coarse classifications and short-lived authentication nonces. URLs contain random capability tokens, not names or email addresses. Cloudflare and the email provider process traffic under their own policies.
- D1 does not persist raw IP addresses or full user agents. The rate limiter uses a daily salted hash without putting it in event records. Worker request observability is disabled; consider infrastructure logs independently.

## Default retention

- Local and remote raw tracking events: 90 days, pruned by the runner and Worker scheduled handler respectively.
- Tracking and unsubscribe token registrations: 180-day validity. Expired unsubscribe pages direct recipients to reply to the original email.
- Per-message first/latest/total signal aggregates and CRM/message records remain until the owner erases them. After raw-event deletion, aggregates can cover a longer interval than the raw-event report.
- Sessions expire after 12 hours; expired session/rate-limit rows are pruned by the runner.
- Authentication nonces expire after ten minutes and are pruned daily.
- Backups: set a deliberate policy, recommended 30 days for routine copies, and include encrypted off-host storage. This policy is operational rather than silently deleting backups on the user's behalf.

## Recipient rights

Every outreach message includes reply-based opt-out instructions. Exact unsubscribe/stop requests from a known prospect are suppressed when synchronized. With Cloudflare configured, a capability-bearing page requires a POST confirmation, preventing link scanners' GET requests from opting someone out. Refreshing remote opt-outs is a prerequisite to sending while active public links exist.

The owner can set Do Not Contact immediately and cancel pending reminders. Suppression is enforced at record import, confirmation and immediately before SMTP. Reimporting, editing status or deleting a prospect does not automatically remove an existing suppression.

Use prospect history to inspect associated records and the Erase action to revoke remote tokens, delete remote events and erase local conversations/CRM data. Erasure first suppresses contact; it waits for any send already in progress and for remote deletion to succeed. Remote deletion must succeed before local mappings are lost. A minimal suppression email and reason are retained to prevent recontact; disclose this purpose and assess its retention/legal basis. Audit records retain non-body identifiers and actions. Also delete or expire affected backups and provider mailbox copies according to your policy; the application does not erase original Hostinger mail.

Handle access/correction requests through authenticated owner controls and appropriate identity verification. Never publish exports or share raw tokens as proof of identity. If someone affirmatively opts back in, review that request outside the routine status editor; v1 deliberately has no one-click suppression bypass.
